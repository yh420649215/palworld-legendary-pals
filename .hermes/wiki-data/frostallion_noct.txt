{{Palpedia|Guardian deity of Palpagos Island known as the Night Caller. In the past, when a calamity struck the land, it soared into the sky and sealed away the threat by casting the island into eternal darkness.}}
'''Frostallion Noct''' (Japanese: '''グレイシャドウ''' ''Glashadow'') is the {{i|Dark}}-[[Elements|element]] subspecies of {{Icon|Frostallion}}. Frostallion Noct is largely identical to the regular Frostallion, except for the fact that it has a black body, a purple mane, tail and hooves, and gold-colored accents.

==Other Characteristics==
Frostallion Noct's only [[Work Suitability]] is {{I|Gathering}}, being the only Pal besides {{I|Braloha}} to naturally have that skill at Lv 4. Though Braloha is much easier to obtain, Frostallion Noct's much higher movement speed allows it to move between different plantations much faster. Additionally, its lack of any other Work Suitabilties means that it won't get distracted by other tasks, making it the perfect dedicated Gathering Pal.

Frostallion Noct also comes with the [[Legend]] and [[Lord of the Underworld]] [[Passive Skill|Passive Skills]], which gives it a boost to its {{I|Attack}}, {{I|Defense}}, movement speed, and Dark-element damage.

==Trivia==
* Prior to version [[0.4.11.0]], Frostallion Noct could only be obtained via breeding.
* Frostallion Noct's Japanese name is a combination of "glacial" (Frostallion's Japanese name) and "shadow."
* Frostallion Noct is #088 in Paldeck on PocketPair [https://x.com/Palworld_EN/status/1799048669314072583 X].
* Unlike the regular Frostallion, Frostallion Noct can gain the [[Passive Skills|Lucky]] Passive Skill. This is because one of its parents, {{Icon|Helzephyr}}, can pass on the Lucky skill, allowing Frostallion Noct to receive boss-specific Passive Skills that Frostallion cannot.
