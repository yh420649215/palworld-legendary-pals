'''Jetragon''' (Japanese: '''ジェッドラゴン''' ''Jedragon'') is a {{i|Dragon}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Jetragon is a draconic Pal with a predominantly light blue body, a white underbelly, red markings on its forearms and thighs, and white markings on its shark-like tail. Jetragon also has two angular black horns with red highlights as well as a large black protrusion on its back with four orange spikes on it. Attached to each of these spikes are large pinkish diamonds made of energy that resemble wings.

==Partner Skill==
As a Legendary Pal, Jetragon is a powerful asset in both combat and exploration. Its [[Partner Skills|Partner Skill]], '''Aerial Missile''', lets it attack with a rapid-fire missile launcher when mounted. While this attack deals relatively low damage per shot, the sheer amount of missiles fired means that it's bound to deal heavy damage to anything it hits. However, it should be noted that Jetragon's Partner Skill prevents the player from using [[weapons]] while riding it.

==Other Characteristics==
Jetragon's only [[Work Suitability]] is {{Icon|Gathering}} at Lv 8, though by the time most players obtain one, much better options are available.

Jetragon also comes with the [[Legend]] and [[Divine Dragon]] [[Passive Skills]], boosting its {{Icon|Attack}}, {{Icon|Defense}}, movement speed, and Dragon-element damage.

Jetragon is also the fastest Pal in the entire game, boasting a sprint speed of 3300. While this makes Jetragon the best Pal for getting around the [[Palpagos Islands]] quickly, {{Icon|Xenolord}} is generally considered to be the superior mount due to it allowing players to use weapons while mounted and having three times the stamina while only being ever so slightly slower than Jetragon (having a sprint speed of 3240).
