'''Necromus''' (Japanese: '''べイントール''' ''Banetaur'') is a {{i|Dark}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Necromus is a large, centaur-like Pal with a black body and a large dark purple tail. Its humanoid upper body and four legs are covered in black armor with red highlights on the torso and helment and dark purple highlights on the leg armor. It also has a large, wing-shaped red plume atop its helmet. In place of hands, Necromus has two large lances made of red crystal and rimmed in dark purple metal at their bases.

==Other Characteristcs==
Necromus has Lv 2 {{Icon|Lumbering}} and {{Icon|Mining}}, though by the time most players obtain one, much better options for both these tasks are available.

Necromus also comes with the [[Legend]] and [[Lord of the Underworld]] [[Passive Skills]], boosting its {{Icon|Attack}}, {{Icon|Defense}}, movement speed and Dark-element damage.

==Trivia==
* Necromus' English name derives from "necro" (from the Ancient Greek νεκρός (''nekrós'')), meaning "dead" or "corpse."
** Its Japanese name is a combination of '''ベイン''' ''bein'' (meaning "bane") and "centaur".
