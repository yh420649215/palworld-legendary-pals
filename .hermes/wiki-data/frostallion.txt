{{Palpedia|Guardian deity of Palpagos Island, known as the Winter Caller. In the past, when a calamity struck the land, it soared into the sky and sealed away the threat by casting the island into an eternal winter.}}
'''Frostallion''' (Japanese: '''グレイシャル''' ''Glacial'') is an {{i|Ice}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Frostallion is a large, horse-like Pal with a white body, a grey mane and tail, and icy shards on its hooves, legs, and forehead. It also possesses a pair of large white wings.

Frostallion also has a {{i|Dark}}-element subspecies, {{I|Frostallion Noct}}.

==Other Characteristics==
Frostallion's only [[Work Suitability]] is {{I|Cooling}}, being one of the few Pals to naturally have that skill at Lv 4. Though {{I|Whalaska}} is easier to obtain, Frostallion's lack of other work suitabilities means that it won't get distracted with other tasks, making it the perfect dedicated cooling Pal.

Frostallion also comes with the [[Legend]] and [[Ice Emperor]] [[Passive Skill]]s, giving it a bonus to its {{I|Attack}}, {{I|Defense}}, movement speed, and Ice-element damage.

As an Ice-element Pal, Frostallion cools down any players near it, making it useful in hot areas like [[Mount Obsidian]] or the [[Desiccated Dunes]].

==Trivia==

* Frostallion's English name is a combination of "frost" and "stallion."

* Frostallion may be inspired by [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus Pegasus], a winged horse from Greek mythology.
* Frostallion is the only Legendary Pal to have a subspecies.
