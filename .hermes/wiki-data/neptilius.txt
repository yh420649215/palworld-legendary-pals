'''Neptilius''' (Japanese: '''ネプティオス''' ''Neptius'') is a {{i|Water}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Neptilius is a large, whale-like Pal with a predominantly grey body and a white and blue underbelly. It has multiple azure gemstones with golden rims on its back, pectoral fins, and tail fin. Neptilius also has a golden "crown" resembling antlers as well as another large azure gem on its head, along with six long, flowing "tentacles" seemingly made of hair.

==Partner Skill==
Neptilius is easily the best aquatic mount in the game. Its [[Partner Skill]], '''Sentinel of the Great Sea''', lets it attack enemies with spear strikes while active, gives it the ability to leap high into the air while swimming, and increases its swimming speed depending on its [[Condensation]] level.

==Other Characteristics==
Neptilius' only [[Work Suitability]] is {{Icon|Watering}} at Lv 4. However, {{Icon|Jormuntide}} and {{Icon|Ghangler}} also have Lv 4 Watering and are much easier to obtain, making Neptilius pretty redundant as a base Pal.

==Trivia==
* Neptilius is the first legendary Pal to be added to the game since its early access release.
* Neptilius' English name derives from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neptune_(disambiguation) Neptune], the Roman sea god, and the planet famous for its blue color.
** Its Japanese name also derives from ''Neptune.''
