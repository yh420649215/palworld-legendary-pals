'''Paladius''' (Japanese: '''セイントール''' ''Saintaur'') is a {{i|Neutral}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Paladius a large, centaur-like Pal with a predominantly grey body and a large yellow tail. Its humanoid upper body and four legs are covered in azure-colored armor with golden highlights. It also has two large, wing-shaped adornments on its helmet and two smaller wing-shaped adornments on the knees of its rear legs. In its right hand, Paladius carries a large azure shield rimmed in gold and with a golden emblem on the front. In its left hand is a large lance made of blue crystal and rimmed in gold at the base.

==Partner Skill==
As a Legendary Pal, Paladius is a powerful asset in both combat and exploration. Its [[Partner Skills|Partner Skill]], '''Holy Knight of the Firmament''', lets it perform a triple jump while mounted, letting it scale most forms of rough terrain with ease. It also increases Paladius' movement speed depending on its [[Pal Condensation|Condensation]] level.

==Other Characteristics==
Paladius has Lv 2 {{Icon|Lumbering}} and {{Icon|Mining}}, though by the time most players obtain one, much better options for these tasks are available.

Paladius also comes with the [[Legend]] and [[Celestial Emperor]] [[Passive Skills]], boosting its {{Icon|Attack}}, {{Icon|Defense}}, movement speed, and Neutral-element damage.

==Trivia==
* Paladius currently has the highest base {{Icon|Defense}} stat among all Pals in the game.
* Paladius' English name derives from the word "paladin."
** Its Japanese name is a combination of '''セイント''' ''seinto'' (meaining "saint") and "centaur".
