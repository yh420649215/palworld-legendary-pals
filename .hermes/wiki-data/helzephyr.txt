'''Helzephyr''' (Japanese: '''ヘルガルダ''' ''Hellgaruda'') is a {{i|Dark}}-[[Elements|element]] [[Pals|Pal]]. Helzephyr is a large, avian Pal with a predominantly black body, jagged, crescent-shaped wings, and blue thunderbolt patterns on its wings and tail. Helzephyr also has a blue face and a wispy purple flame emanating from its head. The markings and flame also glow faintly in the dark.

Helzephyr also has a {{i|Dark}} / {{i|Electric}}-element subspecies, {{i|Helzephyr Lux}}.

==Obtaining==
<tabber>
|-|Habitat=
Helzephyr can be found in the [[Crescent Moon Shore]], the [[Verdant Brook]], and the [[Scars of War]] at night.

Helzephyr is aggressive and will attack the player on sight. Additionally, as it is a Dark-element Pal, it does not sleep at night.

==Partner Skill==
Helzephyr is a good mid-to-lategame combat and exploration mount. Its [[Partner Skills|Partner Skill]], '''Wings of Death''', boosts the player's {{Icon|Attack}} and applies Dark-element damage to the player's attacks while mounted.{{Partner Skill Scale|Wings of Death|1_effect_type=Convert Attack to Dark Element|1_target=Player|1_0_value=1|1_1_value=1|1_2_value=1|1_3_value=1|1_4_value=1|2_effect_type=Attack|2_target=Player|2_0_value=+5%|2_1_value=+7%|2_2_value=+10%|2_3_value=+14%|2_4_value=+20%}}

==Other Characteristics==
Helzephyr's only Work Suitability is {{Icon|Transporting}} at Lv 3, making it very useful until the player manages to obtain Pals like {{Icon|Wumpo}} or {{Icon|Knocklem}}. It is also naturally nocturnal, allowing it to work constantly without needing to sleep.

==Trivia==
* Helzephyr's English name is likely a combination of "Hel" (the Norse goddess of death) and "zephyr" (a Greek term that means "west wind").
** Its Japanese name is a combination of '''ヘル''' ''heru'' (meaning "hell") and '''ガルーダ''' ''Garūda'' (a large mythical bird from Hindu and Buddhist mythology).
